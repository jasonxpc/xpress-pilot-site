Send your files

Upload your files in three steps.

Get your file ready, send it to our secure folder, and we'll confirm by email. Not sure your file is ready? Send it anyway — we'll check it.

1

Get your file ready

  • Send one PDF with the pages in order.
  • Booklets and books need a multiple of 4 pages (8, 12, 16, 20…).
  • Print-ready files include bleed and crop marks. Colors in CMYK.
  • Made it in Canva? Download as PDF Print with crop marks and bleed turned on.
  • Only have a photo, a sample, or a rough idea? That works too — we can help.
